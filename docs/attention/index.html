Each lesson runs Puzzle → Explore → Name → Challenge (interactive-educator method), for a learner who knows linear algebra, softmax and basic neural networks but is new to NLP. It follows the Word Embedding Lab. Source deck: M. Samiei, "Lecture 3: Attention", CS 677, Sharif University of Technology (after Bahdanau, Cho & Bengio 2015 and Vaswani et al. 2017). The worked numbers in lessons 2, 3 and 6 are the slides' own examples (slides 21, 25–26, 54), and the BLEU scores are the papers' (slides 29, 63). Everything else is a teaching toy: the fixed-vector encoder in lesson 1 stores random word codes, the scorer and token vectors in lessons 3–4 are hand-set rather than trained, and lesson 7's toy self-attention uses 2-D embeddings.
