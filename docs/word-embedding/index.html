Each lesson runs Puzzle → Explore → Name → Challenge (interactive-educator method), for a learner who knows linear algebra, softmax and basic neural networks but is new to NLP. Source deck: M. Samiei, "Lecture 2: Word Embedding", CS 677, Sharif University of Technology (after Mikolov et al. 2013, Bojanowski et al. 2017, Caliskan et al. 2017). Word vectors here are 2-D teaching toys: hand-placed in lessons 1, 4 and 5, and trained in your browser on a four-sentence corpus in lesson 3. The Persian sentences are made up, except the Mazandarani ones from slide 3. The n-gram vectors in lesson 6 are random, so that only the shared pieces make words similar. The WEAT scores in lesson 7 are the slide's fastText Persian Wikipedia numbers (slides 33–34); the larger groups in its challenge are hypothetical.
