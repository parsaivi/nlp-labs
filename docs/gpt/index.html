Each lesson runs Puzzle → Explore → Name → Challenge (interactive-educator method), for a learner who knows linear algebra, softmax and basic neural networks but is new to NLP. It follows the Pretraining Lab. The deck's history of OpenAI and Alec Radford (slides 1–13) is not turned into lessons. Source decks: M. Samiei, "Lecture 6: GPTs" and "Lecture 5: Pretraining" (slides 96–107), CS 677, Sharif University of Technology (after Radford et al. 2018, 2019; Brown et al. 2020; Holtzman et al. 2020; Lambda 2020).
